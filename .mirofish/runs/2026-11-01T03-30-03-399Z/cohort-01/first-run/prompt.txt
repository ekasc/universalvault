UniversalVault is a macOS menu-bar app that stores structured personal records encrypted on this Mac. Control+Option+V opens a search palette over whatever app you are in; search a field, press Return, and the value is typed into the focused field. An item can be marked protected, which seals its fields under a second key and asks for Touch ID before they are read; the palette shows a sealed count and unlocks them with Touch ID. Editors autosave and show a Saved badge. An item can be marked for agents, which lets a local command print its non-sensitive fields as JSON. The vault opens at launch without a prompt. Sleep and screen lock seal protected items only; signing out or quitting closes the whole vault. There is no cloud sync.
